About YKE Studios

Who we are and what we stand for.

Our Story

YKE Studios is an independent mobile app development studio founded by Yusuf Kenan Ekizoğlu in Hatay, Turkey. We build clean, focused applications that solve real everyday problems — with a strong emphasis on simplicity, performance, and thoughtful design.

Our Mission

We believe great apps don't need to be complicated. Our mission is to craft mobile experiences that are intuitive to use, well-designed, and genuinely useful — apps that people actually enjoy opening every day.

What We Build

We currently focus on Android applications, crafting each one with care from the ground up — from the user experience to the smallest UI detail. But we're not stopping there. As we grow, we plan to expand to iOS and explore new territories, including mobile gaming. We're a small studio today with a big roadmap ahead.
