General Questions

Is my data stored online?

No. All YKE Studios apps work completely offline. Any data you enter is stored only on your device and never sent to any server.

Why am I seeing ads?

Our apps are free to use and supported by ads via Google AdMob. You can opt out of personalized ads at any time through your device's Google Settings → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization.

How do I delete my data?

You can delete all locally stored data at any time by uninstalling the app. Some apps also offer an in-app deletion option — check the App FAQ below for details.

How can I contact support?

Send us an email at the address shown below. We typically respond within 24–48 hours.

App-Specific Questions

Wishtide
Interactive Story Game · iOS & Android

Why do I have to wait for new letters?

The village runs on real time, so a letter written today may not arrive until tomorrow. Answering a letter also pushes the well's clock forward, which means a chain of replies can often be played through in one sitting. When your letter box is empty, the app tells you when the next one is due.

The well says it is tired and I cannot help. What now?

Helping spends the well's power, and power refills on its own over a few hours. Offerings left by villagers also feed it. Refusing, keeping, or misleading costs nothing — running low is part of the story, not a paywall.

How do I remove the ads?

Open the main menu and tap "Remove ads" — a one-time purchase that removes them permanently. If you reinstall or switch to a new device, tap "Restore purchases" on the same screen; there is nothing to pay again.

Can I undo a decision, or start over?

Decisions are permanent by design — the villagers remember what you chose, and that is the heart of the game. To begin a new story, uninstall and reinstall the app; on Android you can also use Settings → Apps → Wishtide → Clear Data.

Does Wishtide need an internet connection?

No. The whole story is on your device and plays offline. Internet is used only to load ads and to process an in-app purchase.

Which languages does Wishtide support?

Turkish, English, German, French, Spanish, Italian and Portuguese — every letter is fully translated, not just the menus. The app follows your device language.

MoneyHub — Expense & Budget
Personal Finance & Budget Manager · iOS & Android

Do I need an account to use MoneyHub?

No. MoneyHub works fully offline without any account or sign-up. Just install and start tracking.

Is my financial data safe?

Yes. All your data is stored only on your device and never sent to any server. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes everything.

What can I track with MoneyHub?

You can track recurring subscriptions (Netflix, Spotify, etc.) and daily income/expense transactions, with category grouping and monthly overviews.

Does MoneyHub work without internet?

Yes, MoneyHub is fully offline-first. Internet is only used to load ads and to verify purchases.

What is MoneyHub Plus?

MoneyHub Plus is an optional upgrade that unlocks advanced insights (unused-subscription detection, month-end forecast, budget pace), a weekly spending summary, and renewal & budget notifications. It is available as a monthly or annual subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase. A separate one-time "Remove Ads" purchase is also available.

Is there a free trial?

Yes — the annual plan includes a 7-day free trial. If you don't cancel before the trial ends, it automatically converts to a paid annual subscription. You can cancel anytime during the trial to avoid being charged.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through your store account, not inside the app. On iOS: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → MoneyHub. On Android: Google Play → profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → MoneyHub. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Open MoneyHub, go to the paywall (Settings → MoneyHub Plus) and tap "Restore Purchases" while signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account used for the original purchase.

How do refunds work?

All purchases are handled by Apple or Google. Refund requests must be made through the App Store or Google Play under their respective refund policies.

Convert Everything — Converter
Unit Converter · iOS

What categories of units are supported?

Convert Everything covers a wide range of categories including length, weight, temperature, volume, speed, area, time, and more — all in one place.

Does the app work offline?

Yes. All conversions are performed locally on your device. No internet connection is required to use the app.

Does the app collect any of my data?

No. Convert Everything does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Ads are served by Google AdMob — see our Privacy Policy for details.

Fill Up — Time to Water
Daily Water Tracker · iOS & Android

How do I set my daily water goal?

Open the app, go to Settings, and enter your target under "Daily Goal". You can use the suggested amount based on your weight or set a custom value.

How do I enable drink reminders?

In the Reminders tab, toggle on "Reminders" and choose your preferred notification interval and active hours.

How do I reset my daily progress?

Your progress resets automatically at midnight each day. You can permanently delete all data via Settings → Delete My Account.

What is the streak shield?

If you miss your goal one day, you can watch a short ad to protect your streak. The shield keeps your streak alive for that missed day.

One Button Lies
Game / Arcade · Android

How do I play?

Each round shows you a single instruction — TAP, HOLD, DOUBLE TAP, or WAIT. Follow it before the timer runs out. One wrong move and the game ends. The rules get trickier as you progress.

Is my score saved?

Yes. Your top scores are saved automatically on your device. You can view the leaderboard from the game over screen. No account or internet connection needed.

How do I reset my scores?

Go to your device Settings → Apps → One Button Lies → Clear Data. This will reset all scores and progress.

The game feels unfair — I pressed correctly but still lost. Why?

That's the core mechanic. Some rules are designed to contradict what you expect based on previous rounds. If it felt wrong, that's intentional — read the instruction carefully each time, not just the button.

Rituel — Rise & Rest
Morning & Evening Routines · iOS

How do I create a routine?

Open the app, choose a morning or evening ritual, and add the steps you want to follow. You can reorder, edit, or remove steps at any time.

How do I enable reminders?

In each routine's settings, set the time you'd like to be reminded and toggle notifications on. Rituel will nudge you to start your ritual at that time.

Is my data saved to the cloud?

No. Your routines and progress are stored only on your device. No account is required.

How do I reset my routines?

You can delete individual routines from within the app, or remove all data by uninstalling the app.

Kind Skies : Tend a Tiny Tribe
Game / Cozy · iOS & Android (coming soon)

How do I play?

You are the sky above a tiny tribe. Drag the rain cloud over your fields to water the crops, move the sun across the sky to ripen the harvest and heal the sick, and use the wind to dry flooded land. Keep your villagers fed and happy so the village grows. Watering and good harvests earn faith, which you can spend on blessings, relics, and cosmetics. Work through the campaign, then try the endless mode for a longer challenge.

Is my progress saved? Does it sync between devices?

Yes — your progress (stars, faith, unlocks, relics, achievements, and the memorial) is saved automatically on your device. There is no account or cloud sync, so progress stays on the device where you play it. If you delete the app, that data is removed with it.

How do I reset my progress?

There is no in-app reset. To start completely fresh, delete the app and reinstall it — this clears all saved progress on the device. (Note: a one-time "Remove Ads" purchase can be restored later and is not lost by reinstalling.)

I bought "Remove Ads" — how do I restore it on a new device?

Open the main menu and tap "Restore Purchases." As long as you are signed in with the same Apple ID used for the purchase, your Remove Ads upgrade will be re-applied. Optional rewarded ads remain available even after removing ads.

How are ads and privacy handled?

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. You can choose whether to allow personalized ads, and you can change that choice anytime from the privacy options on the main menu. See our Privacy Policy for details.
