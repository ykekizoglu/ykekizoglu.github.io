Frequently Asked Questions

How do I set my daily water goal?

Open the app, go to Settings, and enter your target under "Daily Goal". You can use the suggested amount based on your weight or set a custom value.

How do I enable drink reminders?

In the Reminders tab, toggle on "Reminders" and choose your preferred notification interval and active hours.

Is my data stored online?

No. Fill Up works completely offline. All your data (name, height, weight, water records) is stored only on your device and never sent to any server.

How do I reset my daily progress?

Your progress resets automatically at midnight each day. You can permanently delete all data via Settings → Delete My Account.

Why am I seeing ads?

Fill Up is free to use and supported by ads (Google AdMob). You can opt out of personalized ads in your device's Google Settings → Ads.

What is the streak shield?

If you miss your goal one day, you can watch a short ad to protect your streak. The shield keeps your streak alive for that missed day.
