Privacy Policy

How we handle your data — transparent and straightforward.

Effective date: April 4, 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to all mobile applications published by YKE Studios. It has been prepared in accordance with the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK, Law No. 6698) and Google Play's developer policies. By using any of our apps, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Data Controller

The data controller responsible for your personal data is:

  • Name: Yusuf Kenan Ekizoğlu (YKE Studios)
  • Location: Hatay, Turkey
  • Email: yke.devstudios@gmail.com

Data Stored on Your Device

Some of our apps may ask you to enter information (such as your name, height, or weight) to personalize your experience. This information is stored only on your device and is never transmitted to our servers or shared with any third party. You can delete it at any time by uninstalling the app.

Apps that do not require personalization collect no user data whatsoever.

Advertising Data (Google AdMob)

All of our apps use Google AdMob to display advertisements. We do not directly collect or process this data — it is handled entirely by Google LLC. AdMob may collect:

  • Advertising ID (GAID) and similar device identifiers
  • IP address and approximate location (country/region level)
  • Device information (model, OS version, language)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)
  • App usage data related to ad delivery

This data is processed under Google's own privacy policy. We do not have direct access to it.

Purpose and Legal Basis

The data collected via Google AdMob is used solely to display relevant advertisements within the app. The legal basis for this processing is our legitimate interest in providing free apps supported by advertising revenue (KVKK Article 5/2-f), and where required by law (e.g. in the EU/EEA), your explicit consent.

Third-Party Services

Our apps use Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service provided by Google LLC. We encourage you to review Google's policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Opt out of personalized ads

You can opt out of personalized advertising at any time through your device settings (Google Settings > Ads > Opt out of Ads Personalization).

International Data Transfer

Data collected by Google AdMob may be processed on Google's servers located outside of Turkey. Google LLC complies with applicable international data transfer frameworks. For more information, refer to Google's Privacy Policy.

Data Retention

We do not retain any personal data on our own systems. Data collected by Google AdMob is retained according to Google's own policies. Any data stored locally on your device is retained until you uninstall the app.

Children's Privacy

Our apps are not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through one of our apps, please contact us so we can take appropriate action. Our use of AdMob is configured to comply with applicable child-directed content regulations (COPPA).

Your Rights (KVKK Article 11)

Under the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law, you have the following rights:

  • The right to learn whether your personal data is being processed
  • The right to request information about the processing
  • The right to learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used accordingly
  • The right to know the third parties to whom data is transferred
  • The right to request correction of incomplete or inaccurate data
  • The right to request deletion or destruction of personal data
  • The right to object to processing
  • The right to claim compensation for damages arising from unlawful processing

To exercise these rights, contact us at yke.devstudios@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days as required by law.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this Privacy Policy, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
